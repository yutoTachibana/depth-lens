# v1.0 — Google Gemini cross-vendor benchmark (partial)

> Third leg of `depth-lens compare` on the same K-hop graded bench.
> **Bench was truncated by Gemini API free-tier quotas** — full data is not
> available without billing enabled on the project. We saved what we got:
> a single tier-1 cell for `gemini-2.5-flash` that is the only **non-perfect
> accuracy** observed across all three vendors in this run.

## What ran before the quota hit

| Model | Tier | Budget | N | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gemini-2.5-flash | 1 (easy: mod 13, K=3) | 1024 | 16 | **0.75** |

Then: `google.genai.errors.ClientError: 429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` —
`free_tier_requests: limit 20/day` exceeded. The smaller follow-up probe on
`gemini-2.5-pro` failed immediately because Pro has `limit: 0` on the free
tier — it requires billing.

## Why this single cell is interesting

The corresponding cells on Anthropic and OpenAI:

| Vendor | Cheapest model | Tier 1 accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropic | claude-haiku-4-5 | 1.00 |
| OpenAI    | gpt-5-mini       | 1.00 |
| **Google** | **gemini-2.5-flash** | **0.75** |

Wilson 95% CI for 12/16 ≈ [0.51, 0.91]. So with n=16 we can't say
*"Flash is worse than Haiku at tier 1"* with rigor, but we *can* say
the upper edge of the CI doesn't reach 1.00 — which is exactly where
Haiku and gpt-5-mini sat. This is the first apparent vendor gap the
bench produced, and it appeared on the easiest tier.

A full bench with n≥50 and billing-tier API access would resolve this.
For depth-lens v1.0 purposes the finding is: **a free-tier-only smoke
gave us a more interesting signal than the entire $6 Anthropic+OpenAI run**.
The Gemini-2.5-flash easy-tier miss is the *only* depth-lens-detectable
inter-vendor signal in this benchmark.

## To complete this

1. Enable billing on the Google AI Studio project that owns the key.
   ([console.cloud.google.com → APIs & Services → Enable billing])
2. Re-run the same command:

   ```bash
   export GOOGLE_API_KEY=...
   depth-lens compare \
       --models gemini:gemini-2.5-flash,gemini:gemini-2.5-pro \
       --task custom:runs/bench.jsonl:first_int \
       --depths 1,2,3,4 --compute 1024,4096,16384 \
       --n-samples 16 --plot gemini_bench.png \
       --save-json gemini_bench.json
   ```

3. Expected spend with billing: ~$0.60 (Gemini is the cheapest of the
   three vendors per token).

## What this tells us about depth-lens

The framework picked up a one-cell anomaly that we would have completely
missed in a single-number leaderboard. The investigation-worthy delta
(flash 0.75 vs the other two cheap-tier models at 1.00 on easy
arithmetic) is exactly the kind of fact depth-lens is built to surface —
*even on a partial probe*.
